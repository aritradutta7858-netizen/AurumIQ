1. Purity & Quotation Normalization

MCX quotes gold contracts in heterogeneous lot sizes and purity standards. Direct price comparison is an optical illusion:

P_norm = (P_quoted / Q_basis) × (999.9 / Purity_ppt)
  • GOLDM: Quoted per 10g, 995 purity → Factor: $(P / 10) \times (999.9 / 995)$
  • GOLDTEN: Quoted per 1g, 999 purity → Factor: $(P / 1) \times (999.9 / 999)$
  • GOLDGUINEA: Quoted per 8g, 999 purity → Factor: $(P / 8) \times (999.9 / 999)$
  • GOLDPETAL: Quoted per 1g, 999 purity → Factor: $(P / 1) \times (999.9 / 999)$

2. Zero Look-Ahead Bias Guarantee

Standard backtests suffer from look-ahead bias by computing rolling means or standard deviations using centered windows or full-sample statistics. AurumIQ strictly enforces causality:

μ_t = mean(S_{t-W} ... S_{t-1}) σ_t = std(S_{t-W} ... S_{t-1}) Z_t = (S_t - μ_t) / σ_t

Trade execution occurs on day $t+1$ at opening price, guaranteeing zero information leakage from future bars.

🔄

3. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Mean Reversion

Spread dynamics are modeled as a continuous-time mean-reverting process:

dS_t = θ (μ - S_t) dt + σ dW_t

Using discrete Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) $\Delta S_t = \alpha + \beta S_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$, the mean-reversion speed $\lambda = -\beta / \Delta t$ yields the half-life:

Half-Life (t_{1/2}) = ln(2) / λ

Pairs with half-life between 3 and 25 trading days are optimal for statistical arbitrage.

5. Liquidity Filter & DTE Decay

Low liquidity contracts suffer wide bid-ask spreads and severe slippage. AurumIQ computes a composite Liquidity Score (0–100) combining:

  • Turnover Weight: 40% (Daily ₹ Turnover relative to contract average)
  • Bid-Ask Spread Proxy: 30% (High-Low intraday volatility ratio)
  • Days-to-Expiry (DTE) Decay: 30% (Linear penalty for DTE < 5 days due to delivery margins and physical settlement rolls)
⚠️

6. Model Assumptions & Limitations

To ensure complete institutional transparency:

  • Basis Risk: Delivery center differentials (Ahmedabad vs Mumbai delivery vaults) are assumed uniform in daily settlement prices.
  • Margin Requirements: SPAN + Extreme Loss Margin (ELM) of ~10-12% is assumed maintained without margin calls.
  • Synthetic Data Tagging: Synthetic datasets are strictly stamped with DataSource.DEMO to prevent accidental live capital deployment.
